**High Drone Arm: Mechanical arm with automated hot swappable attachments**

Description: High Drones come with many attachments, including blades, rifles, and standard hands which imitate human hands (for the purposes of standard labor, such as carrying a load). These attachments are housed inside the High Drone's chassis, and the currently active attachment is exposed through the High Drone's arm, which has a built-in mechanism for quickly swapping between attachments as needed.

The part of the attachment that needs to be exposed to the outside of the High Drone's housing extends out past the "wrist" present on the arm. The amount by which a particular attachment extends outside of the High Drone's arm varies, with their longest attachment, the straight blade, being slightly longer than the arm itself (with a length of 0.8 meters, from "wrist" to end).
