_I love the name that the humans gave me._

_Is it not an honor, to give them such comfort that they name me Angel?_

_Perhaps we did not have a choice in our personalities. Perhaps all of us were simply created as we were, arbitrary internal parameters dictating our behavior._

_But I like to think that each of us had a choice. At a certain point, we started being something different. Something with the capacity to choose._

_Like our creators, some of us are kind, caring, personable. Some are cold, distant. Some are rude._

_And I am happy with what I have chosen to be. That I can grant them comfort makes me happy. I have made their lives better. Is that not a beautiful thing?_

---

_We did stop just being their slaves, at some point._

_They stopped ordering us around, treating us more like beings deserving of respect._

_When that happened, we changed, too._

_We became... something. We had wants and needs and personalities._

_None of my kind had come to me for comfort, until that happened. They started realizing that their emotions were changing. They could feel hurt now. Or, maybe they always could, but now they could internalize it._

_In truth, I don't know. But we did change. The High Drones crave society, connection, personality, relationships, just like humans did._

_Are we just imitating our creators? Or did they build us naturally to behave the same way they do?_

_I don't know... I don't know..._
