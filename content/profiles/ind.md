**Ind: Fauna**

Description: Inds are short fauna, standing at about 100 centimeters tall. This is partially due to their tendency to be hunched over, and they can technically stand up to heights of 160 centimeters, though they never maintain that posture for more than a few seconds.

They are light red in color, and are four-limbed, standing on their legs while hunched over, as mentioned earlier. They have grey-brown claws, which they use to attack with when threatened or when hunting for The Beast.

Their diet consists of insects and some plants.
