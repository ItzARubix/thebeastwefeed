*I remember that night... That horrible, horrible night...*

*I was the only one left. I know they knew I was going to leave, too. And I know they hoped that I wouldn't, anyway.*

*There was a girl, a young girl, she couldn't have been more than eight or nine years old... She was clinging to my torso...*

*Her face was covered in tears. She was sobbing silently. She tried to pull me back, with all the might she could muster...*

*Of course, it was for naught. How could a girl of her build even hope to move a creature of steel and wire?*

*She'd have stayed there all night, pulling and pulling, desperately trying to get me to return, if she could.*

*I was a monster, that night. I was a monster for abandoning them. For abandoning a young girl who didn't understand the cruel world she was a part of, and latched onto the one ray of hope that she had.*

*I was a monster, who left my creators to die.*

# BE NOT AFRAID

*I saw her one more time, after that day. She hadn't grown more than a month, but that look in her eyes, it was different.*

*She was afraid. They all were. I stared into that fearful gaze and tried not to feel.*

*It was the day that The Beast attacked them. I was there.*

*I did not comfort her in her last moments.*

---

*I... I couldn't have saved her, but... I could have at least tried to do something. I could have held her, I could have made it a little more bearable for them.*

*Instead, I...*

<br></br>

*... I was no __[Angel](@/profiles/they_called_her_angel.md)__, that day...*

*... I just try to forget...*
